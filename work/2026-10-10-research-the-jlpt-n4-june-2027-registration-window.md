**JLPT N4 mid-2027 in India: registration window and form**

**The 2027 dates aren't out yet, and there is no June sitting.** India holds JLPT only in July and December. The mid-year test you mean is the July test, which is held on the first Sunday of July. **Form steps** (JALTAP's portal is the most detailed one I could see; MOSAI's is similar):
1. **Create an account with identity verification.** JALTAP requires a check through DigiLocker (they recommend this one, and you must tick Aadhaar on the consent screen) or an Aadhaar OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile. Have your Aadhaar and that phone with you.
2. **Fill in your personal details:**
   - full name exactly as on your ID
   - date of birth and gender
   - nationality and native language
   - mobile number and email
   - permanent address, city and state
3. **Choose the exam:** level N4, your test city or centre, and the July 2027 session.
4. **Answer the standard JLPT background questions:** where you studied Japanese, how many hours, why you're taking the test, and so on.
5. **Upload two files:**
   - **Passport-style photo:** JPG, 10 to 100 KB, 3.5 x 4.5 cm, plain background, recent, no selfies.
   - **Scan of a government photo ID showing your date of birth:** Aadhaar, passport, PAN, driving licence or Voter ID, as a JPG of 10 to 100 KB.
6. **Pay the fee online** (UPI, net banking or card) and keep the transaction number, because applications without one are rejected.
   - The N4 fee is quoted at roughly Rs 1,200 to 2,000 depending on the centre. Sources conflict, so check the official 2027 test guide.
7. **Download the test voucher (admit card)** in late June or early July.
   - On exam day, bring the same ID you registered with.
   - Results usually come in late August or September. Use the registration number and passcode from the portal to check them on jlpt.jp.

**Get ready now:** a compliant photo, an ID scan, working DigiLocker access, and a rough count of the hours you've studied.

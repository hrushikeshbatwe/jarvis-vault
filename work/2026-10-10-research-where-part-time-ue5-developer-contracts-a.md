# Research where part time UE5 developer contracts are usually found (job boards, studios, freelance platforms) and list the best options for a February start, with short notes on each.

**Where to look for part time UE5 contracts for a February 2027 start**

Most part time UE5 contract work comes from three kinds of places: game job boards, freelance marketplaces, and contacting studios directly. Studios usually post January and February work in November and December. Pitches you send to studios before the holiday slowdown also tend to get answers. The best time to start applying is late October to mid December.

**Game industry job boards (best for real studio contracts)**
1. **Hitmarker**: The biggest games job board. You can filter by Contract, Freelance, Part time and Remote. UE contract rates seen there are about $30 to $80 an hour. Set up an alert on the free tier.
2. **Work With Indies**: Small indie studios, many remote and on contract, and a lot of UE5 roles. Some listings say US only, so read the location line.
3. **Unreal Engine Forums, Job Offerings section**: Studios and small teams post paid UE gigs here, often part time. Read only the "[PAID]" studio posts and ignore revenue share ones.
4. **GameJobs.co, Games Jobs Direct, Grackle HQ**: Good extra boards with mostly Europe and UK roles. They sometimes have short UE5 contracts of up to 8 weeks.
5. **LinkedIn Jobs**: Search "Unreal Engine" with the filters Contract and Part time plus Remote, and save the search. Many Indian studios and outsourcing houses post here.

**Freelance and vetted platforms**
6. **Upwork**: Has the most UE5 part time listings, many under 30 hours a week and some lasting 6+ months. There is lots of competition and some low budget clients. A strong profile with a demo reel matters, and applying costs Connects.
7. **Lemon.io**: Vetted marketplace that offers both part time and full time work. UE5 roles there pay about $24 to $75 an hour, and contracts average 9+ months. The entry screening is tough but it's free to apply.
8. **Arc.dev / Toptal**: Vetted remote contract platforms that pay well and are open to developers in India. Their screening takes a few weeks, so start now if you want something by February.
9. **Contra**: You get hired through your profile and pay no commission. It works best if you have a polished UE portfolio.
10. **Fiverr / Freelancer.com**: Small fixed price UE jobs like blueprints, VR demos and archviz. Rates are low, but they're a quick way to get your first reviews.

**Studios and direct outreach (India friendly)**
11. **Outsourcing and co-dev studios** like Lakshya Digital, Juego Studios, Gametion, Ubisoft Pune/Mumbai, Technicolor Games, Keywords Studios and 1518 Studios. They hire contract UE developers for client projects, often for a fixed number of months. Watch their careers pages and LinkedIn.
12. **Non game UE5 work** in virtual production, archviz, automotive configurators and XR installations. This field has lots of short part time contracts, for example "UE5 Developer: Immersive Installation (Remote)" on IT Job Board UK. Search "Unreal Engine" together with "archviz" or "virtual production".
13. **India specific listings**: Fawkes Games recently listed a remote Senior Unreal Engineer (contractor) role in India. Also check Instahyre, Naukri (filter to contract) and Built In Hyderabad.

**Communities (where many gigs never reach the boards)**
14. **Discord servers** like Unreal Slackers (the biggest UE community, with a #jobs channel) and Game Dev League. Post a short "available from Feb, part time, UE5 C++/Blueprints" message along with your reel.
15. **X/Bluesky and r/gameDevClassifieds**: Indie devs post paid contract work here. Check for scams and avoid unpaid "rev share" offers.

**Suggested plan**
- This week: Set up saved searches on Hitmarker, Work With Indies and LinkedIn. Apply to Lemon.io and Arc, since vetting takes time.
- November: Fix up your Upwork profile, post in Unreal Slackers, and email 5 to 10 outsourcing or co-dev studios saying you're available from February.
- December: Apply hard to postings for January and February starts.

Some listings I found are dated June to September 2026, so check that a role is still open before applying. I didn't sign up for anything or apply anywhere. If you'd like, I can draft a profile blurb or a studio pitch email for you to approve.
